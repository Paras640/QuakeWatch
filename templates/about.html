About QuakeWatch India

An advanced real-time seismic monitoring and data analytics platform. It bridges live global seismic sensor feeds with local geospatial analysis and modern Fluent UI visualization.

End-to-End System Architecture

Stage 1 • Ingestion

USGS FDSN API

Queries live GeoJSON earthquakes feed with dynamic latitude, longitude, radial distance, and time filters.

Stage 2 • Backend

Flask REST Microservice

Exposes analytical endpoints with caching, resilient offline fallback, and modular Jinja2 template rendering.

Stage 3 • Presentation

Fluent 2 & Leaflet

Delivers dark/light mode mica glassmorphism, responsive data tables, live maps, and instant CSV downloads.

Tectonic Setting of India

The Indian tectonic plate is driving continuously northward into the Eurasian continental plate at a rate of approximately 4 to 5 cm per year. This intense tectonic collision has formed the Himalayas and makes the northern, north-eastern, and western boundaries of India some of the most seismically active regions on Earth.

Seismic Zone Intensity Level (MSK) Zone Factor (Z) Coverage & Vulnerable Regions
Zone V IX and above (Very High) 0.36 Entire North-East (Assam, Meghalaya, etc.), parts of J&K, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Rann of Kutch (Gujarat), Andaman & Nicobar.
Zone IV VIII (High) 0.24 Remaining Himalayan foothills, Delhi NCR, Chandigarh, Sikkim, northern parts of Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and West Bengal.
Zone III VII (Moderate) 0.16 Goa, Kerala, Gujarat, coastal Maharashtra (including Mumbai), parts of Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and West Bengal.
Zone II VI or less (Low) 0.10 Parts of central and southern peninsular India (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Raipur, etc.), historically deemed stable.

Earthquake Safety & Emergency Helplines

1. During an Earthquake

  • DROP to the floor before the shaking knocks you down.
  • Take COVER under a sturdy desk or table.
  • HOLD ON until the ground stops shaking.
  • Stay away from glass windows, exterior walls, and overhead lighting.

2. After the Shaking Stops

  • Expect aftershocks which may trigger further collapse.
  • Evacuate calmly using staircases — never use elevators.
  • Check for gas leaks and turn off the main gas/electrical switch if safe.
  • Move to open areas clear of power lines and damaged structures.
